MOBILE APP DEVELOPMENT

AMA District 14 Member App

Prepared for: AMA District 14

Date: March 18, 2026

Prepared by: Owl Computing Development

Executive Summary

AMA District 14's website—built by Owl Computing last year—has transformed how the district manages memberships, divisions, events, and member communications. Now it's time to put that power in every member's pocket.

We propose building a native-feel mobile application for iOS and Android that gives members instant access to their membership card, event schedules, division news, and more. Designed around the familiar smartphone experience members already know—with widget-style dashboards, push notifications, and a digital wallet for membership cards—this app will become the primary touchpoint between District 14 and its members.

Why a Mobile App?

Your members are at the track, at the trailhead, or on the road. A dedicated mobile app meets them where they are and provides value that a website alone cannot:

Always-On Membership Card

Digital wallet-style membership card accessible even offline. No more forgotten cards at the gate.

Push Notifications

Event reminders, schedule changes, membership renewal alerts, and district news delivered instantly.

Instant Member Lookup

Track officials can quickly verify membership status with a simple search or QR code scan.

Native Experience

Feels like a first-party app with smooth animations, offline capability, and device integration.

Proposed Features

3.1 Core Member Features

  • Home Dashboard: Widget-style home screen with upcoming events, news & notifications, membership status at a glance, and quick-access division icons—inspired by the native iOS/Android widget experience.
  • Digital Membership Wallet: Apple Wallet / Google Wallet-style membership card that members can access instantly. Includes member name, D14 number, expiration date, membership type, and a scannable QR code for gate verification. Accessible offline.
  • Membership Purchase & Renewal: Complete membership signup and Stripe-powered renewal flow directly in the app. Supports Regular, Charter (Club), and Family membership types. Push notification reminders sent before expiration.
  • Division Pages: Dedicated pages for each discipline with division news, event schedules, results & standings links, rules, and social media feeds. Each division represented by its own icon on the home screen.
  • Event Calendar: Full interactive calendar with division color-coding and filtering. Event detail pages with maps, directions, and one-tap "Add to Calendar" integration. ICS export support.
  • Push Notifications: Configurable push notifications for event reminders, schedule changes, membership renewal alerts, and district-wide announcements. Members can choose which divisions they receive notifications for.
  • Member Directory & Lookup: Quick member lookup by name or D14 number. Useful for track officials and fellow members. Shows membership status and active disciplines.
  • Contact & Support: One-tap access to district officers, division contacts, and a built-in contact form.
  • Profile Management: Members can view and edit their profile, manage family members, and view membership history.

3.2 Official & Admin Features (Mobile)

  • Gate Check / QR Scanner: Track officials can scan a member's QR code from the wallet card to instantly verify membership status and type.
  • Quick Member Lookup: Admins and officials can search members by name, D14 number, or AMA number to verify status at the gate.
  • Push Notification Sending: Authorized admins can send push notifications to all members or targeted divisions directly from the app.

3.3 Technical Features

  • Shared Backend: The app will leverage the existing Supabase backend, ensuring all data stays synchronized between the website and mobile app in real time.
  • Offline Support: Membership card, profile, and cached event data available without internet connection.
  • Biometric Auth: Face ID / Touch ID / Fingerprint support for quick, secure app access.
  • Deep Linking: Shared event or division links open directly in the app when installed.

Technical Approach

A key advantage of this project is that the backend already exists. The mobile app will connect to the same Supabase backend that powers the current website, meaning all member data, divisions, events, and content stay perfectly in sync without any duplication or migration effort.

5.1 Technology Stack

Layer Technology
Mobile Framework React Native (cross-platform iOS + Android from a single codebase)
Backend Existing Supabase instance (PostgreSQL, Auth, Storage, Edge Functions)
Payments Existing Stripe integration for membership payments
Push Notifications Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) for Android + Apple Push Notification Service (APNs) for iOS
Maps Google Maps SDK (event locations and directions)
QR Codes Dynamic QR generation + camera-based scanning for membership verification

5.2 Shared Backend Advantage

Because the app connects to the same Supabase backend as the website:

  • Members have one account that works on both web and mobile
  • Admin changes made on the website (events, divisions, content) instantly appear in the app
  • No data migration or synchronization infrastructure needed
  • Existing Stripe payment flows are reused
  • Edge functions (user creation, email, reporting) work as-is

Assumptions

  • AMA District 14 will provide timely feedback and approvals throughout the development process.
  • The existing Supabase backend will remain operational and accessible during development. Any required schema modifications will be coordinated to avoid disrupting the live website.
  • D14 will provide or approve all division icons, branding assets, and content for the app.
  • D14 will provide access to an Apple Developer account and Google Play Developer account for app submission (or Owl Computing can set these up on D14's behalf).
  • Push notification content and cadence will be determined collaboratively during Phase 1.
  • The app is intended as a member-facing tool. Full admin functionality (user management, reporting, email blasts) will remain on the website.
