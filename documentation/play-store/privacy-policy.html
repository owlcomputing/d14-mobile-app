Privacy Policy — AMA District 14 Mobile App

Effective date: July 10, 2026

This policy describes how the AMA District 14 mobile application ("the App"), operated by AMA District 14 ("D14", "we"), handles your information. The App is a member tool for AMA District 14, and it shares its account system and database with the D14 website (ama-d14.org).

Information we collect

  • Account & membership data: name, email address, mailing address, phone numbers, date of birth, emergency contact, AMA/D14 membership numbers, membership type and expiration, and racing disciplines. This is the same membership record D14 maintains for your membership.
  • Payment information: membership payments are processed by Stripe. Your card details are entered on Stripe's secure checkout pages and never pass through or get stored in the App or D14's systems. We retain payment records (amount, date, Stripe transaction reference).
  • Push notification token: if you allow notifications, a device token is stored so D14 can send you event reminders, renewal reminders, and district announcements. You can choose which divisions you hear from, or disable notifications in your device settings.

Camera

The App uses your camera only to scan membership QR codes for verification at events. Camera images are processed on-device and are never stored or uploaded.

How we use information

  • To display your digital membership card and verify membership status
  • To process membership purchases and renewals
  • To send notifications you've opted into
  • To operate district features: event calendars, division pages, member lookup

Member lookup

Signed-in members and track officials can look up members by name or D14 number to verify membership status. Lookup results show name, D14 number, membership status, and disciplines — not contact information.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share data only with service providers needed to run the App: Supabase (database & authentication hosting), Stripe (payments), and Expo (push notification delivery). Each acts on our behalf.

Data retention & deletion

Membership records are retained as part of D14's member administration. To request deletion of your account and associated data, contact webmaster@ama-d14.org.

Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Access to member data is restricted by row-level security and role-based permissions.

Children

Family memberships may include minors added by a parent or guardian. Minor accounts are managed by the parent; we do not knowingly collect data directly from children.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be announced through the App or the D14 website.
